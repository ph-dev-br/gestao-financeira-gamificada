# Gamified Financial Education (single-context)

A prototype gamified financial system aimed at high-school teenagers to teach saving and basic fixed-income investing through personalized challenges, goal tracking, expense recording, and teacher-managed class oversight.

## Language

**Student**:
A teenage user of the system who participates in challenges, records expenses, and pursues savings/investment goals.
_Avoid_: User

**Guardian Consent**:
An explicit permission state from a parent/guardian that authorizes a Student's participation.
_Avoid_: Implicit consent assumption

**Guardian Role Scope (prototype)**:
Guardian exists as a consent artifact for a Student, not as a signed-in platform role.
_Avoid_: Guardian account workflows

**Consent Compliance Model (launch)**:
Guardian consent can be provisionally self-declared and later teacher-verified, without blocking Student feature access during provisional state.
_Avoid_: Hard pre-access consent gating in prototype

**Consent Capture Flow (launch)**:
Students may self-declare guardian consent, but the declaration is provisional until Teacher verification.
_Avoid_: Unverified self-declaration as final consent

**Teacher**:
An educator who manages a class, assigns or monitors challenges, and reviews student progress via the administrative panel.
_Avoid_: Admin (use Teacher for school staff)

**Teacher Class Scope**:
A Teacher may manage multiple Classes concurrently.
_Avoid_: Single-class teacher assumption

**Student-First Naming**:
The canonical term in both domain language and code is Student rather than User.
_Avoid_: Generic user naming for student entities

**Account**:
The authentication identity shared by all signed-in roles, with role-specific profiles such as Student and Teacher.
_Avoid_: Role-specific auth duplication

**Authentication Baseline (launch)**:
Accounts sign in with email/password in the initial prototype.
_Avoid_: SSO-first identity assumptions

**Class**:
A group of Students managed by a Teacher for monitoring and assignment purposes.
_Avoid_: Group, Cohort

**Class Membership Scope (launch)**:
A Student has one active Class membership at a time.
_Avoid_: Multi-class simultaneous enrollment

**Participation Scope (launch)**:
Student flows are restricted to enrolled Students in Teacher-managed Classes.
_Avoid_: Open public student access

**Enrollment**:
The relationship that links a Student account to a Teacher's Class, while keeping the Student as an individual account holder.
_Avoid_: Shared account, Class account

**Join Code Enrollment**:
Students join Teacher-managed Classes using a class join code.
_Avoid_: Open enrollment by default

**Join Code Lifecycle**:
Class join codes follow an expiration/rotation policy and can be regenerated by Teachers.
_Avoid_: Permanent unrotated codes

**Class Transfer Semantics**:
Student personal history remains portable across Class changes, while class analytics are bounded to each enrollment period.
_Avoid_: History loss on transfer

**Join Semantics (launch)**:
A Student joins a Class immediately when providing a valid join code.
_Avoid_: Default pending-approval enrollment

**Challenge**:
A time-boxed, personalized task or series of tasks designed to encourage saving or healthy financial habits (e.g., "Save X this week").
_Avoid_: Quest, Mission

**Challenge Authority Model**:
Challenges are generated in a hybrid mode: the system suggests personalized options, and Teachers can assign or override.
_Avoid_: Single-source challenge control

**Transfer Challenge Rule**:
When a Student transfers Class, class-assigned challenges from the previous Class are closed, while personal goals/history are preserved.
_Avoid_: Cross-class challenge carryover by default

**Challenge Horizon Policy**:
Challenges are weekly by default, with monthly variants for investment-oriented behaviors.
_Avoid_: Horizon ambiguity

**Goal**:
A persistent target a Student works toward (e.g., save for a specific item, reach a savings balance).
_Avoid_: Target

**Dual Goal Model**:
Goals include both monetary outcomes and habit outcomes.
_Avoid_: Money-only success framing

**Expense**:
A recorded outflow of money by a Student, categorized for habit analysis.
_Avoid_: Transaction (use Expense when referring to spending records)

**Monetary Scope (launch)**:
All monetary values are represented in BRL in the initial prototype.
_Avoid_: Multi-currency assumptions

**Language Scope (launch)**:
The prototype experience is in Portuguese for Brazilian high-school learners.
_Avoid_: Multilingual content scope at launch

**Expense Capture Mode (prototype)**:
Expenses are recorded manually by Students in the prototype.
_Avoid_: Mandatory bank integration

**Core Expense Taxonomy**:
Expense categories are standardized across all Students to keep habit analysis and personalization comparable.
_Avoid_: Per-user category drift

**Low-Friction Evidence Policy**:
Manual expense and investment records do not require proof attachment at launch.
_Avoid_: Mandatory receipt workflow

**Fixed income (investment)**:
A low-risk investment vehicle encouraged initially (e.g., government bonds, savings account equivalents).
_Avoid_: Stocks, Variable income

**Investment Tracking Mode (prototype)**:
Students may manually record real fixed-income investments, without brokerage/account integrations.
_Avoid_: In-app trading, Automated execution

**Investment Contribution**:
The canonical investment record: each manual amount a Student allocates into a fixed-income product.
_Avoid_: Generic transaction record

**Contribution-First Investment Scope**:
At launch, investment tracking focuses on contributed amounts, not return or yield calculation.
_Avoid_: Performance-engine assumptions

**Curated Fixed-Income Types**:
Investment contributions are classified using a curated set of fixed-income product types.
_Avoid_: Free-text investment type taxonomy

**Gamification**:
UI and UX elements (points, badges, levels, progress bars) used to motivate and visualize Student progress.
_Avoid_: Marketing features

**Educational-Only Product Boundary**:
The prototype is strictly educational and non-commercial, with no subscriptions, ads, or financial-product upsell.
_Avoid_: Monetization-first mechanics

**Non-Monetary Reward Economy**:
Gamification points represent educational progress only and are not redeemable for monetary value.
_Avoid_: Cash-equivalent reward mechanics

**Behavior-Based Leaderboard**:
Leaderboard ranking compares habit and challenge consistency signals, not absolute money values.
_Avoid_: Wealth-based ranking

**Pseudonymized Class Ranking**:
Student leaderboard identities are shown as pseudonyms to classmates.
_Avoid_: Real-name social comparison by default

**Leaderboard Ranking Rule**:
Class ranking orders Students by consistency score, using streak as a tiebreaker.
_Avoid_: Money-based ordering

**Streak-First Feedback Loop**:
The primary motivational loop is consistency streaks: weekly for expense recording and monthly for investment contributions.
_Avoid_: One-off completion scoring as the main loop

**Streak Grace Policy**:
A Student has one grace miss per month before a streak is reset.
_Avoid_: Zero-tolerance streak resets

**Backdating Window**:
Students may backdate manual financial records up to 7 days.
_Avoid_: Unlimited retroactive logging

**Backdated Streak Equivalence**:
Backdated entries inside the allowed window count the same as on-time entries for streak purposes.
_Avoid_: Penalized late-record distinction

**Metric Separation Rule**:
Streak and consistency score are distinct metrics with different meanings.
_Avoid_: Collapsing habit metrics into a single indicator

**Teacher Panel / Admin Panel**:
An interface where Teachers can create classes, enroll Students, assign Challenges, and view class-level analytics.
_Avoid_: Super-admin interface

**Teacher Visibility Boundary**:
Teachers see educational progress summaries (totals, progress, and habits) rather than raw per-student sensitive financial details.
_Avoid_: Full financial surveillance

**Organizational Scope (launch)**:
The prototype is designed for a single-school context.
_Avoid_: Multi-tenant school assumptions

**Teacher Core Analytics View**:
The primary teacher dashboard view emphasizes class consistency trends, at-risk student count, and challenge completion trends.
_Avoid_: Money-first class analytics

**MVP Surface Scope**:
The initial product surface includes Student experience and Teacher panel only.
_Avoid_: Guardian portal scope at launch

**Personalization**:
Adjusting Challenge difficulty, recommendations, and messaging based on Student profile and behavior.
_Avoid_: Generic content

**Primary Adaptation Signal**:
At launch, challenge personalization is driven mainly by spending-category patterns and goal-progress state.
_Avoid_: Opaque black-box scoring

**Personalization Explainability**:
Each personalized challenge includes a plain-language reason for why it was suggested.
_Avoid_: Unexplained personalization decisions

**Personalization Cadence**:
Challenge suggestions refresh weekly and after major goal changes.
_Avoid_: Unbounded real-time personalization churn

**Primary Outcome Metric**:
The key prototype outcome is improvement in Student habit consistency over time.
_Avoid_: Wealth accumulation as the main KPI

**At-Risk Inactivity Threshold**:
A Student is considered at risk after 14 days without qualifying activity.
_Avoid_: Undefined inactivity status

**Qualifying Activity**:
An expense record, challenge check-in, or investment contribution that indicates active participation.
_Avoid_: Passive app open as activity

**At-Risk Intervention**:
When a Student becomes at-risk, the system sends a Student nudge and surfaces a Teacher notification.
_Avoid_: Silent at-risk state

**Coaching Tone Policy**:
Student-facing prompts and alerts use supportive, non-shaming coaching language.
_Avoid_: Guilt-driven engagement messaging

**Notification Channel Scope (launch)**:
Student nudges and Teacher alerts are delivered in-app only.
_Avoid_: Multi-channel notification complexity at launch

**Consistency Scoring Baseline**:
Qualifying activity types have equal weight in launch-phase consistency scoring.
_Avoid_: Opaque weighted scoring without explanation
